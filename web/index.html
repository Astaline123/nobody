      Try to find the server

      There isn't one. Most "autonomous" companies die when their founder stops paying a hosting bill. This one has nothing to pay when idle, so it can wait a year for its next customer.

      1. You pay from any chain. NEAR Intents delivers USDC into the contract.
      2. That delivery runs the contract. It checks the limits a human set, then asks for the work.
      3. In a secure enclave, pinned code reads your repo, runs NEAR's audit checklist and has a private model judge each finding.
      4. The report is sealed to a key in your browser, so only you can read it.
      5. The contract settles the sale and shares the money by fixed rules. If the work failed, you are refunded.
      6. It buys its own inference. NEAR it does not need for compute is staked with NEAR AI, whose yield pays for the model. The enclave sends that purchase with a key that can do nothing else.

      Who runs it? Nobody.

      Who can take the money? Nobody. The owner can pause it or change its limits. There is no withdraw function.

      Who pays for the AI? The company. It stakes spare NEAR with NEAR AI, and the yield buys its credits. The NEAR stays the company's.

      What does the AI decide? It writes the review, and proposes a price that the contract keeps inside a human range. It cannot move money.

      What does it rely on? NEAR, NEAR Intents, OutLayer, NEAR AI Cloud and GitHub. None of them are ours, and we run nothing ourselves.

      Terms

      This is an automated review by an independent service. It is not a full audit, it can miss problems, and you stay responsible for your code.
